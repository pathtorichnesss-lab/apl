Slide 04 — Tone of Voice & Design Rules
Brand Personality

Voice & Rules

🗣️ Tone of Voice
Warm & Encouraging
Talk like a knowledgeable friend, not a distant expert. "You've got this" energy.
Aspirational & Grounded
Dream big but stay real. Show possibility without bypassing the work.
Educational & Empowering
Every word should leave the reader feeling smarter and more capable.
Feminine & Inclusive
Celebration of women building wealth — never gatekeeping, always welcoming.
✦ Design Rules
1
Always use the brand palette — no off-brand colours unless for specific data visualisation.
2
Rounded corners everywhere. Bubbly and approachable, never harsh or angular.
3
Mix Playfair italic with Nunito bold for visual contrast and editorial feel.
4
Sticker illustrations add personality — use sparingly and consistently across assets.
5
Generous white space — let the design breathe. Premium brands never crowd their canvas.
